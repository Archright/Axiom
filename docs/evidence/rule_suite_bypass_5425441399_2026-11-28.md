### §2.2 Two attempts — and the trap firing for real

The rotation is a four-step sequence: grant on `27097366`, grant on `27525972`, REST merge pinned to the
head, restore from a `finally`. Snapshots are the full `GET` body minus server-owned keys
(`id`, `source*`, `created_at`, `updated_at`, `node_id`, `_links`, `current_user_can_bypass`), so the
restore rewrites exactly the fields the policy owns.

**Attempt 1 — aborted at step 1, nothing merged.** The grant decision was read from the `PUT` response
body, whose shape does not carry the field the check looked for, so the script treated a successful grant
as a failure and exited before the merge. The `finally` still ran:

## §0. What this document records

PR #525 taught `mint-publish` not to restore the A-layer (native toolchain) cache it never executes,
via a new opt-in caller input `skip-a-restore` on `.github/actions/setup-axiom-toolchain/action.yml`
set at the single `mint-publish` call site in `.github/workflows/certificate-mint.yml`, plus the two
`certificate-mint.yml@` pin rotations in `.github/workflows/admission-gate-v2.yml` that carry that
change into the calling engine. Three of those five paths are members of the App trust-surface manifest
(`config/policy/trust_surface_manifest.json`, 17 paths as of this record), so App `5795152`
(`axiom-admission-verifier`) refused the PR by design and the normal merge queue was structurally
unavailable for the landing. The MUST-T2a delegation was registered as a PR comment **before** the merge
command; a temporary bypass was granted on the two rulesets carrying required checks, the landing was
performed through the REST merge endpoint pinned to the PR head, and the rulesets were restored
byte-identically. This document fixes those facts, the ledger row, and the invariant preservation.
